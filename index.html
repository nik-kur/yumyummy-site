Why we built it

Made by someone who kept quitting calorie apps.

“Logging a restaurant meal accurately? Basically impossible.”

You search “chicken shawarma” and get fourteen results, none from the place you ate. You guess the portion, guess the side, and three minutes later you've logged something you don't trust. Five times a day. By Friday the app is collecting dust.

YumYummy was built by an ex-McKinsey manager who lived that cycle for years, eating at airports, client sites and hotels in a different city every week. Every tracker was either fast but inaccurate, or accurate but painfully slow.

So this one was built from scratch to be both: the tracker its creator actually wanted to use, every meal, every day.

Questions

How accurate is it? AI calorie counts are usually wrong.

That's why YumYummy doesn't guess first. It looks for real nutrition data from USDA, official labels, manufacturers and restaurant menus. When no official data exists, it estimates and tells you so: every entry is labeled Exact, Estimate or Approximate, and you can tap through to the source or edit any portion.

I've tried calorie trackers before and always quit. Why would this be different?

Most trackers take three to five minutes per meal: searching, scrolling, adjusting servings. YumYummy takes about ten seconds. Type it, say it or snap it. When logging is that quick, sticking with it stops being a willpower problem.

How much does it cost?

You start with a free trial. The price for your country is shown in the App Store before you subscribe. Subscriptions are billed by Apple, and you can cancel anytime in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Does it work for restaurants and local food?

Yes, that's where it shines. If a chain, café or brand publishes nutrition data online, YumYummy finds it. For a dish with no published data, it breaks the meal into ingredients and shows you exactly what it assumed.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. YumYummy is available for iPhone.

What happens to my data and photos?

Your diary is private and we don't sell your data. Photos and voice notes are used to log your meal. You can delete your account and all your data in the app under Profile → Delete account. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

I used the YumYummy Telegram bot. What now?

YumYummy now lives in the iPhone app, and the Telegram bot is no longer active. If you have a question about an earlier Telegram subscription, write to us via the Support page.
